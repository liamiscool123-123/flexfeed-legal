FlexFeed Support

Help, safety and contact

Contact us: liamd298@gmail.com
We reply within 2 business days, and review safety reports within 24 hours.

Report a post, comment or account

  • Post: tap ⋯ at the top right of the post → Report post or Report account.
  • Comment: long-press the comment → Report comment.
  • Reports are anonymous. The person you report isn't told who reported them.

Delete a post or comment

Tap ⋯ on your own post → Delete, or long-press your own comment → Delete comment. Deleting a recipe also removes remakes other people posted of it.

Block someone

Tap ⋯ on their post (or long-press their comment) and choose Block. You won't see each other's posts, recipes or comments, and they can't follow you. To unblock: Profile → Blocked accounts.

Delete your account

Profile → Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm. This permanently deletes your profile, photos, posts, recipes, comments, logs and XP right away. If you can't get into the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Can't log in?

On the login screen, tap No password or forgot it? Email me a code, and enter the 6-digit code we send you. Guest accounts that were never saved with an email can't be recovered on a new phone.

Nutrition numbers look wrong?

Macros are entered by users, or come from Open Food Facts when you scan a barcode, so they're estimates. You can edit the numbers before you post. FlexFeed isn't medical advice. See our Terms.

If you're struggling

If you're having thoughts of self-harm, or struggling with eating, you're not alone. In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). For eating-disorder support, see the National Alliance for Eating Disorders at allianceforeatingdisorders.com. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.
