Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026

FlexFeed is a social app for sharing meals and recipes and tracking macros. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and how you can delete everything. "We" means FlexFeed and its operator, Liam Davis.

Short version: we collect what you post and what you need to use the app. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app at any time.

Location

If you tap "Use my location", your phone gets your position once and converts it to a place name on the device (on iPhone this uses Apple's geocoding service). We save only the country, state/region and city names. We never store your coordinates or track your location in the background.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to take meal photos and scan food barcodes. A photo leaves your phone only when you post it. Barcode scanning happens on your phone; only the barcode number is sent to look up the product.

Public by default

Your username, profile, posts, recipes, comments, XP and leaderboard ranks are visible to other users. If you turn on Private account, only followers you approve can see your posts and recipes. Your username still shows on leaderboards. Your email, macro logs, blocks and reports are never shown to other users.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: accounts, feed, recipes, remakes, streaks and leaderboards.
  • To keep people safe: review reports, remove content and accounts that break our Terms, and prevent spam and cheating.
  • To make regional stats (for example average protein by state). These only use groups of at least 20 people, and no individual is identified.
  • To contact you about your account (for example login codes). We don't send marketing email.

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, posts, recipes, comments, logs, XP, follows, likes, saves, blocks and reports are deleted right away. Copies may remain in encrypted server backups for up to 30 days before they are overwritten. Reports other people filed about your content may be kept for moderation records.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete account. It's permanent and immediate.
  • Delete content: tap ⋯ on your own post to delete it, or long-press your own comment. To change your username, email us.
  • Make your account private: Profile → Private account.
  • Location: type your city instead of using GPS, or turn off location for FlexFeed in iPhone Settings.
  • Access or correct your data: email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have extra rights: to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object to certain uses. Email us to use any of them. We won't treat you differently for doing so.

6. Children

FlexFeed is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

7. Security

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access is restricted per user. Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text. No system is perfectly secure, so use a password you don't use anywhere else.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll also tell you in the app before they take effect.
